# Low CSAT

In Delight AI agent, the low customer satisfaction (CSAT) feature helps you identify conversations where customers rated one or two on the CSAT survey. When a customer submits a low score, the AI agent automatically analyzes the conversation and assigns up to two categories that explain the likely cause of dissatisfaction.

## Filter low CSAT conversations

1. Navigate to **Evaluate** > **Conversations**.
2. Click the **CSAT** filter dropdown and select **Low CSAT**. The list updates to show only conversations with a score of one or two.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/CSAT_score_clicked.png" alt="CSAT filter dropdown with Low CSAT selected" width="800"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. When the **Low CSAT Category** filter appears, select one or more categories to narrow the results.

<figure><img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/CSAT_category_clicked.png" alt="Low CSAT Category multi-select filter with category options" width="800"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Optionally, apply **AI Confidence** or **Date Range** filters for additional filtering.

The trend chart remains visible when the **Low CSAT** or **AI Confidence** filter is applied, showing the conversation volume over time for the selected filter conditions.

---

## View low CSAT insights

Click a conversation in the filtered list to open its detail view. In the **Insights** panel on the right, you can review the following:

- **Low CSAT Category**: Up to two AI-assigned categories, displayed in order of impact.
- **Low CSAT Reason**: A text summary of why the customer was dissatisfied, generated by the AI agent.

These fields appear only for conversations with a CSAT score of one or two.

## Low CSAT categories

The AI agent classifies low CSAT conversations into the following five categories. A single conversation can have up to two categories, listed in order of impact.

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Inadequate compensation | The customer was dissatisfied with the compensation offered, such as coupons or refunds. |
| Inadequate response | The AI agent's response did not adequately address the customer's question. |
| Escalation refusal | The customer declined the offer to connect with a human agent. |
| Situation frustration | The customer was frustrated by external factors such as delays or service quality issues. |
| Others | The conversation does not fall into any of the above categories. |

{% hint style="info" %}
The low CSAT classification is available only for conversations created after this feature was released.
{% endhint %}
